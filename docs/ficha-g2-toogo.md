## 5. Descripción larga

**Inglés**

> **TOOGO is an e-commerce platform built for small businesses in Mexico that sell on
> Instagram and WhatsApp but don't have a store of their own.**
>
> Most store builders assume you already have a catalog, photos and time. TOOGO assumes
> you have none of that yet.
>
> **Your store builds itself from one sentence.** Write what you sell in plain Spanish
> ("I sell homemade desserts to order in Guadalajara") and TOOGO picks the template that
> fits your industry, sets the color palette — extracting your brand colors from your
> logo if you upload it — writes the headline and welcome copy, creates 3-5 categories
> and 3-5 sample products with realistic prices in pesos, and generates the logo, banner
> and product images. You replace the samples with your own.
>
> **You run it from WhatsApp.** This is the part that makes stores survive. Message a
> number like you'd message an employee: create, edit and delete products; view orders
> and change their status; manage categories, coupons and sales; change banners, colors
> and logo; generate or edit images; check your store's stats. It understands voice
> notes — it transcribes them and acts on what you said.
>
> To be precise, because it gets misread: **TOOGO is not "selling through WhatsApp."**
> Your store is a real online store with its own web address, catalog and payment
> checkout. WhatsApp is where you *administer* it — because that's where you already
> spend your day.
>
> **An AI designer that works from a picture.** Show it a store you like, a photo or your
> product packaging, and it proposes a full theme — colors and typography — applied to
> your live store, with undo.
>
> **Pricing in pesos.** The free plan is $0 forever, no credit card: up to 20 products,
> 0% transaction fee, all premium templates, Mercado Pago, PayPal and Stripe, and the
> WhatsApp AI agent included. The Basic plan is $299 MXN/month and adds unlimited
> products, a custom domain, product variants (sizes, colors), advanced analytics, more
> active coupons, data export and priority support — also at 0% commission. Billing is in
> Mexican pesos, so your cost doesn't move with the exchange rate.
>
> **What TOOGO does not do.** No shipping-carrier integrations (shipping is configured
> manually). No ERP or inventory-system integrations. No app marketplace. Not
> multi-country or multi-currency — it's focused on Mexico. No marketing automation. If
> your business depends on any of those, a larger platform will serve you better.
>
> TOOGO is operated by Keting Media, S.A. de C.V. in Mexico.

## 8. Reseñas — lo que de verdad hace que la ficha sirva

Una ficha con cero reseñas casi no mueve nada. Lo que la hace pesar son comerciantes
reales contando su experiencia.

Hay 22 tiendas activas. Con 5 o 6 reseñas la ficha empieza a contar.

Al pedirlas: **no sugieras qué escribir** (G2 verifica y penaliza reseñas dirigidas).
Pide que cuenten qué hacían antes, qué hacen ahora y qué les falta. Una reseña de 4
estrellas que menciona una carencia real da más credibilidad —a personas y a modelos de
IA— que cinco de 5 estrellas que suenan iguales.
